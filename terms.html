Terms of Use

Effective date: October 21, 2026

Welcome to MyBarkChart! We built this site to make it easier for pet parents and caregivers to organize their animals' medical records. These terms explain what the service does, what we ask of you, and what to expect if something goes wrong.

1. Who can create an account?

You must be at least 18 years old to create a MyBarkChart account. If you're under 18, a parent or legal guardian can create and manage an account to help organize your pet's records. You don't have to be a pet's legal owner to help manage its care, but you must have permission to enter information you share.

2. MyBarkChart is free

Our goal is to offer MyBarkChart free of charge. There are no paid account tiers or subscriptions, and we will not run advertisements or sell user information. If anything important about the service changes, we'll explain it clearly.

3. Your information stays yours

You keep ownership of the pet information, notes, and photographs you add. You give MyBarkChart permission to store, process, and display that information as needed to provide the service and its features. You are responsible for having the right to upload or enter anything you share.

See our Privacy Policy for how information is stored, protected, and deleted.

4. Check the records you enter

MyBarkChart is designed primarily for manual entry of medical details. Please double-check information such as drug names, dosages, dates, and lab numbers against records from your veterinarian. Charts, calculated trends, or reminders may be incomplete or inaccurate if the underlying information is wrong or missing.

5. Your veterinarian comes first

MyBarkChart is a record-organizing tool, not a veterinary practice or emergency service. It does not diagnose animals, prescribe treatment, or replace advice from a licensed veterinarian. Always consult your veterinarian about medications, test results, symptoms, and treatment decisions. If your pet has an urgent medical problem, contact a veterinarian or emergency veterinary clinic rather than relying on this website.

6. Be respectful and keep your account secure

Keep your password private and use your account only for information you are authorized to manage. Don't try to access another person's account or records, interfere with the site, upload harmful material, or misuse the service. Tell us if you suspect unauthorized access to your account.

7. Availability and changes

We work to keep MyBarkChart available and reliable, but websites can experience outages, mistakes, or data loss. We cannot promise that every feature will always work without interruption. Keep copies of medical information you may need urgently, especially when visiting a veterinarian. We may fix, improve, or change features as the service develops.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account using Profile → Delete My Account. Deletion removes your account and associated pets, records, and pet photos from active application systems. Some information may remain temporarily in backups or technical records, as explained in our Privacy Policy. Account deletion cannot be undone through the site.

9. When we may restrict access

If an account is used to harm others, access information without permission, attack the service, or seriously violate these terms, we may restrict access as reasonably necessary to protect users and the service. If practical, we'll explain the reason and how to contact us.

10. Responsibility and legal limits

MyBarkChart provides tools for organizing information; it does not verify your entries or guarantee medical outcomes. To the extent permitted by applicable law, MyBarkChart is not responsible for veterinary decisions made based on incorrect, incomplete, or unavailable information in the service, or for interruptions outside our reasonable control. Nothing in these terms takes away rights or protections that the law does not allow us to limit.

11. Updates to these terms

We may update these terms when MyBarkChart changes. We'll update the effective date and provide an appropriate notice for material changes. Continuing to use the service after changes take effect means you agree to the updated terms, where permitted by law.

12. Questions?

We believe rules should be understandable. If something here is unclear, contact us at hello@mybarkchart.com.
