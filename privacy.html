Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

MyBarkChart helps pet parents organize their animals' medical information. We built it to make pet records easier to access and understand—not to turn your information into a product.

Our promise: MyBarkChart will never sell your information or run advertisements. The service is intended to remain free to use.

1. Information you provide

When you create an account, we collect your email address, a name or username, and information needed to authenticate your account. You may add pets and information such as their names, species, breeds, ages, weights, photos, medications, conditions, lab measurements, vaccinations, clinic and veterinarian details, medical records, appointments, and notes. You decide which optional details to enter.

Please avoid entering information about other people unless it is relevant and appropriate for your pet's care.

2. How we use information

We use the information to create and maintain your account, display and organize your pets' charts, save changes you make, send account-related emails such as verification or password recovery, support account security, and respond when you contact us. We do not use your pet records to sell advertising or sell your data.

3. Where data is stored and who helps deliver the service

MyBarkChart uses Supabase for account authentication, database storage, and private pet-photo storage. The website's public code and pages are served through GitHub Pages. We use Resend to deliver account-related emails. These providers may process the technical information necessary to operate their respective services under their own applicable terms and privacy practices.

Like other online services, our hosting and service providers may handle necessary technical information such as IP addresses, device or browser details, and service logs. We do not represent that all such technical information is stored only in the pet-record database.

4. Access and security

Pet information is associated with your account. MyBarkChart uses authenticated access and database row-level security controls designed to prevent one user from reading or changing another user's pet records. Pet photos are stored in a private storage bucket, with access controls linked to the account.

We take security seriously, but no website or storage service can guarantee absolute security. Protect your password and let us know if you believe your account has been accessed without permission.

5. Cookies and browser storage

MyBarkChart uses browser storage as part of keeping you signed in, remembering which pet's chart is selected, and supporting certain site features. Clearing browser data or using a different device may affect these preferences and your signed-in session. Authentication services may also use browser-based mechanisms necessary to operate securely.

6. How long we keep data and how deletion works

We keep your account and pet records while your account is active, unless you edit or delete information through available features. You can permanently delete your account using Profile → Delete My Account. This action is designed to remove your account, associated pet records, and stored pet photos from the active application systems.

Deletion from active systems does not necessarily mean immediate removal from system backups, technical logs, or records a service provider must retain for legitimate operational or legal reasons. Supabase database backups may retain earlier copies for a limited period before they expire under the applicable backup schedule.

7. Your choices

You can view, add, change, and delete pet information using the features provided in your account. You can also request help or ask privacy-related questions by emailing us at hello@mybarkchart.com.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as MyBarkChart evolves or its service providers change. We will update the effective date when we make changes. Material changes that significantly affect how user information is handled should be communicated to users through an appropriate notice.

9. Contact

Questions about your information or this policy? Email hello@mybarkchart.com.
